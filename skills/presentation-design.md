# Presentation Design Skill (Assertion-Evidence & Human-Crafted Aesthetic)
Adapted from top-rated Agent Skill `jwynia/agent-skills/presentation-design` and Michael Alley's Assertion-Evidence Framework.

## Core Tenets
1. **No "AI Template Slop"**:
   - BAN all random dark neon cards, excessive rounded gradient boxes, artificial pills, and repetitive icon stamps.
   - BAN generic bullet point dumps where every bullet has an empty bold prefix followed by generic text.
2. **Assertion-Evidence Structure**:
   - The headline of every content slide must be a **concrete, complete sentence assertion** stating the conclusion or finding (NOT just a vague topic label like "五、 三单勾稽").
   - The body of the slide must be **visual, quantitative, or tabular evidence** that directly proves the assertion.
3. **True Human-Crafted Aesthetic**:
   - Clean white or off-white backdrop (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`).
   - Deep University Navy (`#0F2C59`), Academic Slate (`#1E293B`), and purposeful highlight color (`#1A56A0`).
   - Generous margins, breathable whitespace, human-designed data cards, and genuine comparative tables.
   - Screenshots must be cleanly integrated like human case-study slides in McKinsey / Big-4 presentations.
